## Open items review (2026-11-05)

- **Partington attrition (12 start, 5 finish):** two further searches found only "five coaches" and "one English Premier League club". The twelve is unconfirmed, so it stays out of the article.
- **Build:** `npm run build` completed with all 13 live articles and 6 tools, so every edited MDX file compiles. Pages were not re-viewed one by one in the browser.
- **Voice scan (live articles):** no "worth sitting with", "here's the thing" or "the whole point". The filler-word count is inflated by the title "How Much Should You *Actually* Say?" in cross-links, and the real uses are few. The "isn't X, it's Y" construction appears about ten times across `fundamentals-of-soccer-coaching` (3), `7v7-soccer-formations`, `coach-without-stopping-the-game`, `scanning-and-receiving` and `teach-it-or-let-the-game-teach-it`. Em dashes run high only in `how-much-should-you-actually-say` (13) and `coach-without-stopping-the-game` (12). None was edited.
- **Real-life angle:** the articles already carry a few real lines (the old mentor's five rabbits, "I watch a lot of other coaches' sessions"). Nothing further can be written without stories from Chris, per the no-invented-experience rule.

## Read-through

The critique's three structural points hold up: the same few studies carry several articles, the limits sit well below the headline, and there is no note that the site is tied to PitchLabs software. Its most reliable findings are the Buszard and Hintermann ones, both confirmed. The Cushion and Harvey points are largely wrong, because the site had already checked both against the PDFs. The critique was written without that record.
